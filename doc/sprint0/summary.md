## 1. Project Objective

FreshRoute is a web-based grocery ordering platform focused exclusively on fresh and organic food.  

The objective of this project is to design a streamlined system that allows users to browse organic products, manage a shopping cart, and complete an order efficiently. The platform prioritizes simplicity, clarity, and a grocery shopping experience centered on fresh produce and natural food essentials.

---

## 2. Key Users

### Primary User: Health-Conscious Student
A university student who prefers organic and minimally processed foods but has limited time to shop in person.

- Shops weekly or bi-weekly  
- Prioritizes fresh produce and natural ingredients  
- Wants a quick and simple checkout process  
- Is mindful of the total cost before purchasing  

### Secondary User: Busy Working Professional
A working professional who values convenience and reliability when purchasing fresh groceries.

- Orders fresh food regularly  
- Wants intuitive browsing and filtering  
- Expects an efficient and smooth ordering process  
- May revisit previous orders for repeat purchases  

---

## 3. Key Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Weekly Organic Grocery Order
A student browses the Produce and Dairy categories, selects organic vegetables, milk, and whole-grain bread, reviews the cart, adjusts quantities, and completes checkout.

### Scenario 2: Quick Fresh Purchase
A working professional needs a few fresh items for dinner. They quickly filter by category, add items to the cart, review the total, and complete checkout within minutes.

### Scenario 3: Cart Adjustment Before Checkout
A user adds several organic products but removes one after reviewing the total price. They update quantities and proceed with the final order.

---

## 4. Key Design Principles

### 1. Simplicity Over Feature Overload
The platform focuses only on fresh and organic products. The interface should avoid unnecessary complexity and keep navigation clear.

### 2. Clarity and Transparency
Product information, pricing, and cart totals must always be clearly visible so users understand what they are purchasing.

### 3. Focused Scope
FreshRoute will only support fresh and organic groceries to maintain a clear, focused scope for the website.

### 4. Efficient Checkout Experience
Users should be able to complete an order in as few steps as possible, with no confusion.

---

FreshRoute aims to provide a clean, focused, and user-centered experience for purchasing fresh and organic food online.
