## 1. Project Objective

FreshRoute is a web-based grocery ordering platform dedicated to providing fresh, organic, and ethically sourced food products. The platform operates as an online grocery retailer with a centralized warehouse model, sourcing products from trusted suppliers and fulfilling customer orders through scheduled delivery.

The objective of this project is to design a streamlined system that allows users to browse organic products, manage a shopping cart, and complete an order efficiently. The platform prioritizes simplicity, clarity, and a grocery shopping experience centered on organic, fresh produce and natural food essentials.

---

## 2. Key Users

### Primary User: Health-Conscious Individuals
People who prefer organic and minimally processed foods but have limited options from stores near them.

- Shops weekly or bi-weekly  
- Prioritizes fresh produce, natural ingredients, and ethically sourced produce  
- Wants a quick and simple checkout process  
- Is mindful of the total cost before purchasing  

### Secondary User: Busy Working Professional
A working professional who values convenience and reliability when purchasing fresh groceries.

- Orders fresh food on a regular basis  
- Wants intuitive browsing and filtering  
- Expects an efficient and smooth ordering process  
- May revisit previous orders for repeat purchases  

---

## 3. Key Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Weekly Organic Grocery Order
A shopper browses the Produce and Dairy categories, selects organic vegetables, milk, free-range and non-GMO chicken, and whole-grain bread, reviews the cart, adjusts quantities, and completes checkout.

### Scenario 2: Quick Fresh Purchase
A working professional needs a few fresh items for dinner. They quickly filter by category, add items to the cart, review the total, and complete checkout within minutes.

### Scenario 3: Cart Adjustment Before Checkout
A user adds several organic products but removes one after reviewing the total price. They update quantities and proceed with the final order.

### Scenario 4: Pre-made Recipes
Instead of browsing through individual products, patrons can browse dishes. Users provide the number of people being fed with a dish, and the appropriately scaled quantity of ingredients will be added to their cart.

---

## 4. Key Design Principles

### 1. Simplicity Over Feature Overload
The platform focuses only on fresh and organic products. The interface should avoid unnecessary complexity and keep navigation clear.

### 2. Clarity and Transparency
Product information, pricing, and cart totals must always be clearly visible so users understand what they are purchasing.

### 3. Focused Scope
FreshRoute will only support fresh, organic, and ethical groceries to maintain a clear, focused scope for the website.

### 4. Efficient Checkout Experience
Users should be able to complete an order in as few steps as possible, with no confusion.

---

FreshRoute aims to provide a clean, focused, and user-centred experience for purchasing fresh and organic food online.
