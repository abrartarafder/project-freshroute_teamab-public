## 1. Introduction 

Many Canadians rely on meal-prep kits or grocery delivery services for convenience. However, most major platforms prioritize price and variety over fully organic, ethically sourced, and fair-trade options. 
Health-conscious and ethical consumers often struggle to find a service near them that aligns completely with their values. 

Our product focuses specifically on fresh, organic, ethically sources, and fair-trade ingrdients, designed for consumers who prioritize sustainability, transparency, and health.

## 4. Target Users & Scenarios
### 1. Specific Users 
- Health-conscious individuals. 
- Busy working professionals who value convenience and healthy options.
- Ethical and sustainable consumers. 

### 2. Specific Scenarios 
- Health-conscious consumers looking to quickly order fresh, organic food, without having to sort through products and stores.
- Users who care about carbon footprint and fair-trade labor practices, and ethically sourced products.  

## 5. Why Competitors Did Not Focus on These Users and Scenarios
### Smaller Market Size 
The strictly organic/fair-trade market is smaller than the mass grocery market because products are more expensive and not as widely available or demanded. 

### Higher Costs 
Organic and fair-trade products are more expensive to produce and increase logistics costs.

### Limited Inventory & Variety 
Focusing solely on organic/fair-trade products reduces supplier options. 

### Scale Conflicts 
Large companies are focused on scaling operations. For all the previously mentioned reasons, this becomes difficult to do. 

## 5. What It Would Take for Competitors to Pivot 
- Finding new suppliers who can provide organic/fair-trade merchandise.
- Accept reduced margins (less profit per product).
- Reposition branding toward sustainability and ethical consumption.
